Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

Hadith Unlocked is operated by Adnan Mukhtar. This policy explains how we collect, access, use, store, and share information when you use Hadith Unlocked, its Qurʾān reading and memorization tools, personal notebook, and public lookup services. Contact us at adnanmukhtar@gmail.com about privacy or your data.

You can browse and search the public library without signing in. Sign-in and Google Drive connection are optional and support the personal features described below.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

  • Google sign-in: When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile image, when provided by Google. We use these to authenticate you, display your profile, associate saved information with your account, and manage access permissions. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Saved study information: We store the bookmarks, highlights, preferences, likes, reflections, and memorization or review activity you choose to save. This supports your reading experience, review scheduling, progress tracking, and synchronization across devices. Reflections you publish can be visible to other visitors with your display identity; personal notebook notes are separate from public reflections.
  • Notebook information: We process the titles, Markdown text, tags, and source references you save in My Notebook to display, search, edit, and export your notes. The Google Drive section below explains storage and permissions.
  • Technical and usage information: Our servers and infrastructure providers process information such as IP addresses, requested pages and searches, browser or device information, timestamps, and errors. We use this to deliver the service, diagnose failures, monitor performance, and prevent abuse. Google Analytics, where enabled, measures use of the site.
  • Support: If you contact us, we use your contact details and the information you send to respond and investigate your request.
  • Optional paid features: Where payments are enabled, Stripe processes checkout and payment details. We receive and retain customer identifiers, transaction status, and purchase or credit records needed to provide the purchased service. We do not store your full payment card number.
  • Optional recitation feedback: If you choose an available recording feature and grant microphone permission, your recording is sent to our transcription service to produce recitation feedback.

Your notes, reflections, and study activity may reveal religious beliefs or other sensitive information you choose to include. Only save or publish information you are comfortable processing through the service.

2. Google Drive access and notebook storage

Connecting Google Drive is a separate permission step from signing in. The notebook connection requests openid and email to confirm the connected identity and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file to manage files created by, or explicitly made available to, this app. It does not request permission to read your entire Drive, Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

When connected, My Notebook creates a visible Hadith Unlocked folder in your Drive and stores notes as Markdown files. Our server accesses their contents and metadata to list, read, search, create, update, export, and move your notebook files to trash when you request deletion. Your notes are not made public by the notebook connection. Sharing a file yourself through Google Drive is governed by the sharing settings you choose there.

We store your connected Google account identifier, folder and file identifiers, source mappings, and migration status in our database. Authorization tokens for ongoing Drive access are encrypted on our server; Drive access and refresh tokens are not placed in browser storage. Note contents pass through our server when you use notebook features.

If you migrate an existing notebook to Drive, the app copies and verifies your notes before switching active storage to Drive. Original database notes are retained as a recovery snapshot; that snapshot does not track later Drive edits. You can request deletion of retained copies using the contact address below.

3. How we share information

We use service providers to operate the application, including hosting and network security providers, Google for authentication, connected Drive storage and analytics, New Relic for performance monitoring, and Stripe where payment features are enabled. These providers process the information needed for their roles. Public content you choose to post is visible to other visitors. We may also disclose information when required by law or where necessary to investigate abuse or protect users and the service.

We do not sell Google user data or use Google sign-in or Drive data for advertising, credit decisions, or training generalized artificial intelligence models. We use that data to provide the user-facing features described in this policy. Hadith Unlocked’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

If you access our public lookup services from another application, such as an AI assistant, that application handles your conversation and any information you give it under its own privacy policy. Connecting to our public lookup service does not grant it access to your private notebook.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and browser storage for sign-in state, session information, reading preferences, and local interface settings. Google Analytics may use cookies to measure visits and interactions where enabled. You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser; doing so may sign you out or reset saved local preferences. Server-side account information and Drive files are not deleted by clearing your browser.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Account and study records are stored on our servers, and connected notebook files are stored in your Google Drive. Providers may process information in countries other than where you live. We use HTTPS, account-based access controls, and encryption for stored Drive authorization tokens to protect access. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

We retain account and saved study information while needed to provide your requested features, unless you delete it or request deletion. Technical logs, support records, and payment records may be retained as needed for operations, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Deleted information may remain in backups until those backups are replaced; legal or security requirements may require some records to be retained. Migrated notebook recovery copies remain until separately removed, as explained above.

6. Your choices, access, and deletion

  • Use the public library without an account: Sign-in is not required to read or search public material.
  • Manage your information: Use the available controls to edit or remove saved items and export notebook notes. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated data. We may need to verify that you own the account before fulfilling the request.
  • Disconnect Drive: Disconnecting in My Notebook removes the stored authorization tokens. It does not delete your Drive files, the recorded account binding, file mappings, or retained migration copies. Ask us to remove retained server-side data if you no longer want it kept.
  • Revoke Google access: Remove Hadith Unlocked from your Google account connections to revoke its Google permissions. Revocation does not itself delete information already saved by the service.
  • Delete Drive notes: Deleting a note in the app moves its Drive file to trash. You can manage or permanently delete those files in Google Drive. Disconnecting or deleting your Hadith Unlocked account does not automatically delete your Drive files.

Send data and privacy requests to adnanmukhtar@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, or restrict the use of personal information, and to raise a complaint with your local privacy authority.

7. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it as appropriate.

8. Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on this page and change the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or the contact information associated with your account.
