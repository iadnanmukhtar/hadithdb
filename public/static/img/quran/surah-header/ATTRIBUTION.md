# Surah header SVG attribution

## Cartouche border

`border.svg` is derived from [Sura border.svg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sura_border.svg) by Hadysylmy, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Its fill color was changed to black.

## Surah titles

The files in `names/` are derived from RNRCTG's modified `Surah N of 114.svg` files in Wikimedia Commons' [SVG surah headers category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_surah_headers), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The local black encodings were retrieved from the Quran.com [`surahTitleSvg` mirror](https://github.com/quran/common-components/tree/master/utils/surahTitleSvg). Their fill color was normalized to black and each SVG's view box was tightened around the original vector artwork for responsive placement. The vector artwork is otherwise unchanged.

These derived SVG assets remain available under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Basmalah

`basmala.svg` is the unmodified [Basmala.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Basmala.svg) by Baba66, used under [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
