# Tafsir category review — 2026-10-26

All 61 existing tafsir catalog records were reviewed. These are overlapping editorial discovery categories, not judgements of theological correctness, report authenticity, or a hierarchy of spiritual merit. No books were added or removed, and author affiliation fields were not used as classification inputs.

Major means sustained historical influence, foundational source value, or an established teaching/reception tradition. It is not an exhaustive canon; absence is not a negative rating. Categories describe defining characteristics rather than every technique a commentator sometimes uses. Modern means composition from 1800 CE onward, not the date of a new printing or translation. Uncertain dates are left unassigned.

Existing user placements of Easy Tajwid and Asbab al-Nuzul among related aids, and reading/grammar/vocabulary aids under Language & Rhetoric, are preserved. Stable group IDs preserve existing filter links. Selected Works is renamed to describe collected writings; Summarized becomes Concise Tafsirs.

## Category definitions

- **major tafsirs**: Historical influence: foundational sources, sustained scholarly reception or broad teaching and translation traditions. This is an editorial reference category, not a theological endorsement or an exhaustive ranking.
- **encyclopedic**: Extended reference works with broad coverage or large-scale systematic compilation; physical size alone is insufficient.
- **transmitted tafsir**: Early exegetical reports and transmitted interpretation are a defining method; this includes analytical works and does not certify every report.
- **collected opinions**: Organized presentation of alternative exegetical opinions is a defining format.
- **language & rhetoric**: Linguistic, grammatical, rhetorical or reading analysis is a defining emphasis; retained reading and vocabulary aids are included.
- **legal interpretation**: Extended analysis of Quranic legal rulings and juristic arguments is a defining emphasis.
- **quran by quran**: Cross-explanation of Quranic passages is the declared organizing method, not simply an occasionally used technique.
- **quranic coherence**: Relations between verses and surahs form the organizing focus.
- **reflection & guidance**: Reflection, practical guidance and accessible moral lessons form a defining emphasis.
- **concise tafsirs**: Concise explanation, including original concise works and abridgments; does not assert that every work summarizes another book.
- **modern & contemporary**: Period facet for works composed in the nineteenth century CE onward, including modern compilations. Reprints/translations of medieval works do not become modern tafsirs. Undated or unidentified works are not assigned by guesswork.
- **collected exegetical writings**: Later collections drawn from an author’s writings rather than a continuous independently authored tafsir; replaces the evaluative Selected Works label.
- **general tafsirs**: Broad verse-by-verse commentary without one exclusive specialist method; may overlap with historical prominence or period.
- **other works & aids**: Related reference and recitation aids retained in this catalog; not a ranking of scholarly merit.

## Evidence and limits

Memberships are editorial inferences from the identified editions, catalog metadata, and the sources below. Existing catalog descriptions are used for format and identity, not adopted as independent evidence for doctrinal or superiority claims. Individual category membership evidence is stored with the seed. Newly expanded Major memberships rely on the work’s reception rather than its affiliation.

Sources for expanded historical reception assessments:
- [razi](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/al-din-al-razi/): Historically important extensive commentary, independently of agreement with its theology.
- [zamakhshari](https://www.nmc.utoronto.ca/research-publications/faculty-publications/medieval-exegesis-golden-age-tafsir): Central commentary and subsequent gloss tradition in the history of medieval exegesis.
- [baydawi](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/bayzawi-naser-al-din-abul-kayr-or-abu-said-abd-allah-b/): Sustained educational reception and numerous supercommentaries.
- [ibn-atiyah](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ibn-atiyye-el-endelusi): Influence on subsequent exegesis, including Abu Hayyan.
- [abu-hayyan](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/el-bahrul-muhit--ebu-hayyan): Major linguistic reference that generated abridgments and later derivative works.
- [alusi](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ruhul-meani): Extensive synthesis of the earlier exegetical tradition.
- [ibn-ashur](https://zenodo.org/records/12573733): Influential modern commentary; importance is compatible with modern and linguistic memberships.
- [rida](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/dinbil/article/1769584): Influential modern reformist commentary; incompleteness does not negate historical influence.
- [en-maududi](https://idarapublications.com/): Widely circulated and translated modern commentary; inclusion is an editorial reception assessment.
- [thalabi](https://isamveri.org/pdfdrg/D04073/2019_16/2019_16_AYGUNA.pdf): Influence on later exegetes, including al-Baghawi; report reliability is a separate question.
- [nasafi](https://dijital.diyanet.gov.tr/Dergilik/makale/nesefinin-medarikut-tenzil-adli-tefsirinde-arap-gramer-ve-belagatiyla-ilgili-konulara-yapilan?id=36434&productId=6298): Sustained scholarly reception of a concise linguistic commentary.
- [suyuti-t](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ed-durrul-mensur): Historical reference collection for transmitted exegesis.
- [shawkani](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/fethul-kadir--sevkani): Established synthesis combining transmitted reports and analytical interpretation.
- [abu-alsuod](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/ydoguilaf/article/409881): Rhetorical commentary with a substantial manuscript and reception tradition.
- [jalalayn](https://fonsvitae.com/product-tag/jalalayn/): Established concise teaching commentary and translation tradition.
